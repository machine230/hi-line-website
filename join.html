✈️ The Aircraft

Our 1963 Cessna 172 is equipped with a constant-speed prop and a Lycoming O-360 engine overhauled in 2023. She's hangared year-round at KRNT — no tie-downs, no weather exposure, always ready to fly.

🤝 True Ownership

Members purchase a share in the aircraft — you're an actual owner, not a renter. When you leave the club, you sell your share. Monthly dues keep the airplane maintained; the dry rate covers fuel and your portion of variable costs.

🔧 Maintained In-House

We have a licensed A&P mechanic within our ownership group. Squawks get addressed quickly, maintenance stays transparent, and we don't pay shop markups. Safety is the non-negotiable foundation of everything we do.

👥 The Community

Hi-Line is for pilots who value safety, affordability, and genuine community. We fly together, maintain together, and make decisions together. If that sounds like your kind of flying club, we'd love to meet you.
